Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Clearspace version 2.1

Clearspace provides personal planning, focus tracking, optional voice capture, AI thought organization, and CV-to-job comparison. This policy describes the current extension and these static information pages.

Planning data is stored on your device. Optional AI sends the content you choose to analyze to your configured provider. Voice recognition may use Chrome’s online speech service.

1. Data stored on your device

The extension stores tasks, schedules, focus-session records, preferences, voice transcript drafts, saved CV text, job-description drafts, and provider configuration in Chrome’s local extension storage. It does not automatically sync this data to a Clearspace account or server.

Your provider API key is kept in Chrome session storage and page memory. It is not bundled with the extension or included in planner exports. Unsaved CV and job inputs carried to the detailed matcher may also use session storage.

2. Optional AI processing

When you click Organize my thoughts, the extension sends the selected transcript and current date to your configured AI provider. When you click Run job match, it sends the displayed CV and job-description text. Requests also include instructions for the requested feature and your API key for authentication.

The default provider is Hive, using DeepSeek. You may configure another compatible provider; in that case, requests go to the endpoint you selected. AI processing does not start automatically when you capture a thought or save a CV.

Providers receive the submitted content and connection information such as your IP address and process it under their own terms, retention practices, and privacy policies. See Hive’s privacy policy. Review your chosen provider’s policy before use. Clearspace cannot delete copies retained by a provider. Remove private details you do not want to transmit.

3. Voice recognition

Microphone use starts when you click Start listening and grant permission. Chrome’s speech-recognition service may send audio to Google for transcription. Clearspace retains the resulting text draft, not an audio recording. See Google’s privacy policy. Availability and processing depend on Chrome and its speech service.

4. Webpage access and blocking

The extension reads visible job-page text only when you click Read current job page. The captured text and page title may be retained locally as a draft. It does not monitor browsing history or automatically collect the pages you visit.

Optional distraction blocking uses the domains you enter and blocks new top-level page loads during active focus sessions. Selected domains and blocking preferences stay in extension storage.

5. Use and sharing

Data is used to provide features you request. Clearspace has no advertising or analytics integrations in this release. It does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility.

AI content is shared with the provider you configure. Voice audio may be processed by Chrome’s speech service. There is no Clearspace account, subscription, or backend in this version.

6. Retention and deletion

Local extension data remains until you change or remove it, clear extension storage, or uninstall. Use task controls to remove tasks, clear editable drafts, use Remove saved CV to delete the saved CV, and use Forget API key to remove the key from the current page and session storage. Other open pages may retain an in-memory key until closed; close extension pages when removing access.

Chrome clears session storage at the end of the browser session and may also clear it when an extension reloads, updates, or is disabled. Exported files remain wherever you saved them. Uninstalling does not delete provider-held data or those files.

7. Security and your choices

AI endpoints must use HTTPS. You control whether to enable voice, connect a provider, analyze text, save a CV, or enable blocking. Avoid putting confidential information in inputs unless you intend to send it for the requested analysis. API keys are sensitive; do not include them in support messages.

8. This website and support

These pages do not include analytics, cookies, remote fonts, or forms that send data. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs, including IP addresses, according to its own policy. If you contact support, the recipient and support service will receive the information you choose to provide.

9. Policy changes

This policy will be updated when data practices change, including before introducing accounts, subscriptions, or server-side processing. The revision date above identifies the latest update.
