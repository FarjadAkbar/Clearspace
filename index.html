Clearspace Privacy Policy Last updated: October 6, 2026 Clearspace provides personal planning, focus tracking, optional voice capture, AI thought organization, and CV-to-job comparison. Data stored on your device Clearspace stores tasks, schedules, focus-session records, preferences, voice transcripts, saved CV text, and job-description drafts in Chrome’s local extension storage. Your provider API key is kept in Chrome session storage until the browser session ends. Optional AI processing When you click Organize my thoughts, Clearspace sends the selected transcript and current date to your configured AI provider. When you click Run job match, Clearspace sends the displayed CV and job-description text to that provider. The default provider is Hive, using DeepSeek. If you configure another compatible provider, your selected content is sent to that provider instead. Your API key is sent to the configured provider to authenticate requests. These providers process requests under their own privacy policies and retention practices. Clearspace cannot delete copies retained by a provider. Remove private information you do not want to send before starting analysis. Voice recognition Microphone access begins only when you click Start listening and grant permission. Chrome’s speech-recognition service may send audio to Google for transcription. Clearspace saves the resulting text draft, not an audio recording. Webpage access Clearspace reads visible job-page text only when you click Read current job page. It does not monitor browsing history. Distraction blocking uses domains you choose. Use and sharing Data is used to provide the features you request. Clearspace does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. AI content is shared with your selected provider; voice audio may be processed by Chrome’s speech service. Control and deletion You can edit or delete tasks, clear transcript and job-description drafts, remove your saved CV, and forget your API key. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension storage. Exported files and content previously sent to third-party services are not removed by uninstalling. Changes This policy will be updated when Clearspace’s data practices change, including before introducing accounts, subscriptions, or server-side processing.
