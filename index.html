01 / Get it out

A home for your thoughts

Capture tasks, ideas, and notes. Use optional Urdu or English voice input and review your transcript.

02 / Choose what matters

Clarity over urgency

Use the Eisenhower Matrix and choose two daily highlights to give important work your attention.

03 / Give it a place

Plan around your time

Schedule time blocks, set daily capacity, and leave a buffer. Gently reconsider unfinished work.

04 / Begin small

The 25-minute deal

Track focused sessions and breaks. Compare estimates with actual focus time and optionally block distracting sites.

05 / Optional AI

Talk it through

Connect your own Hive key to organize related sentences into distinct thoughts. Review suggestions before saving.

06 / Career preparation

CV-to-job comparison

Compare your CV text with a selected job description. Get an estimated match score, evidence, and gaps—not an employer’s actual ATS score.
